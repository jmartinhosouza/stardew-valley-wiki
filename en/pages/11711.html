The Galaxy Soul is an item used to upgrade Galaxy weapons in the Forge. Weapons forged using Galaxy Souls keep their enchantments and gem upgrades.

  • Galaxy Souls can be purchased at Qi's Walnut Room for data-sort-value="40"> 40 each.
  • After killing at least 50 Dangerous Monsters [1], Galaxy Souls can be purchased from the Island Trader on the last day of a season for Radioactive Bar (10).
  • After killing at least 50 Dangerous Monsters [2], Galaxy Souls can be dropped by any Dangerous Monster.
    • Note that Shadow Snipers and Putrid Ghosts do not count as Dangerous Monsters unless the Mines are at difficulty level 2 ("Danger in the Deep" quest and Shrine of Challenge both active).
  • Semi-transparent Big Slimes containing a Galaxy Soul can be seen in the "Dangerous" Mines or "Dangerous" Skull Cavern.[3] The Galaxy Soul is dropped upon killing the Big Slime.

Forge recipes

See also: Forge

The Galaxy Soul is used in the Forge to upgrade a Galaxy Weapon into an Infinity Weapon. The player must upgrade a Galaxy Weapon 3 times, consuming 3 Galaxy Souls and 60 Cinder Shards in total.
