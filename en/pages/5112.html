Crit. Power

From Stardew Valley Wiki
Jump to navigation Jump to search
For technical details about Crit. Power mechanics, see Combat/Mechanics.

Crit. Power (short for Critical Power) is a Combat statistic. A critical strike unaffected by Crit. Power does 3x base damage. Crit. Power causes this multiplier to increase so that critical strikes do more damage.

Increasing Crit. Power

The following items increase Crit. Power. The effect is permanent when the item is equipped. Forging a weapon with Jades will increase its natural Crit. Power stat by 5 per Jade.
