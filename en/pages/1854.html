Starfruit is a fruit crop that grows from Starfruit Seeds after 13 days. It has the second-highest sell price of any crop in the game, with only the Sweet Gem Berry selling for more. One Starfruit can be purchased each year at the Luau for data-sort-value="3000">3,000g.

Bundles

Starfruit is one of the options in the remixed Dye Bundle on the Bulletin Board.

Recipes

Starfruit is not used in any recipes.

Buildings

Starfruit is required to build a Junimo Hut, purchased from the Wizard's Tower.

Tailoring

Starfruit can be used in the spool of the Sewing Machine to create the dyeable Genie Pants. It can be used in dyeing, serving as a yellow dye at the dye pots, located in Emily's and Haley's house, 2 Willow Lane.

Quests

  • Kent requests a Starfruit by mail on the 15th of Summer (year 2) in the "A Soldier's Star" Quest. The reward is data-sort-value="500">500g and 1 Friendship heart.

History

  • 1.0: Introduced.
  • 1.1: Reduced value to data-sort-value="750">750g, down from data-sort-value="800">800g.
  • 1.4: Can now be used in Tailoring.
  • 1.5: Iridium quality is now possible when using Deluxe Fertilizer. Can now be bought at the Luau for data-sort-value="3000">3,000g. Is now an option in the remixed Dye Bundle.
