The Eggplant is a vegetable crop that grows from Eggplant Seeds after 5 days and continues to produce every 5 days after maturity.

Tailoring

Eggplant is used in the spool of the Sewing Machine with Cloth in the feed to create a dyeable Button Down Shirt. It is a purple dye when used in the spool of the Sewing Machine with a dyeable clothing item in the feed. It can be placed in the purple dye pot at Emily's and Haley's house for use in dyeing.

Quests

  • Eggplant may be randomly requested in Fall at the "Help Wanted" board outside Pierre's General Store for a reward of data-sort-value="180">180g and 150 Friendship points.

History

  • 1.4: Can now be used in Tailoring.
  • 1.5: Iridium quality is now possible when using Deluxe Fertilizer. Is now an option in the remixed Quality Crops Bundle.
