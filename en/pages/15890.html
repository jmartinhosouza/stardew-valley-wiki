A collection of furniture infused with Junimo energy.
Information
Source(s) Traveling Cart for data-sort-value="70000">70,000g
Sell Price Cannot be sold

The Junimo Catalogue is a catalogue of furniture and decor items that can be purchased at the Traveling Cart for data-sort-value="70000">70,000g after completing either the Community Center Bundles or the Joja Community Development Form. There is a 10% chance the catalogue will appear in the shop's inventory.

Once placed, on the catalogue allows the player to obtain unlimited Junimo-themed furniture and decor items at no cost.

Tip: Shift + on an item to add it directly to inventory, instead of attaching it to the mouse cursor.
